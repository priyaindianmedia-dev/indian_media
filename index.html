About Indian Media

A growth partner,
not just an agency.

Indian Media was built on one belief: marketing should pay for itself. Every rupee you invest with us is tracked, measured and pushed to produce leads, sales and real brand equity — never vanity metrics.

From our head office in Mumbai, we plan and execute complete digital growth systems — branding, websites, Meta and Google ads, SEO, content and creative production — under one roof, with one accountable team.

Over 5+ years, we've partnered with 100+ businesses across real estate, healthcare, food, fashion, education and e-commerce, delivering 500+ projects that turned browsers into buyers.
